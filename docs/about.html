Academic Context & Scope

Course: Statistics for Machine Learning (SML)
Curriculum Framework: Project Based Learning (PBL)
Department: B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)
Organization: Code-Crew-Nexus

Geographic Scope: 21 unique physical CAAQMS stations partitioned into a 7-station Hyderabad Metropolitan Panel and a 15-station India Representative National Panel (with 1 overlapping station: PROJ_007 Zoo Park, Hyderabad).

Temporal Study Window: 570 continuous calendar days from 2025-03-01 through 2026-09-21, capturing multiple pre-monsoon, monsoon, and post-monsoon seasonal transitions.

Scientific Limitations

  • Verified-Subset AQI: The Indian AQI computed here represents a conservative verified-subset index derived from PM2.5, PM10, and O3. Gaseous pollutants (CO, NO2, SO2) were excluded because modern OpenAQ API feeds lack dual-unit mass tracking after 2022, preventing independent regulatory verification.
  • Standardized Drift ($D_z$): Drift scores quantify observational mean shifts between temporal windows. They are descriptive indicators and do not establish causal atmospheric emission attribution.
  • Seasonal Prevalence Shift: Fixed-threshold models experience sensitivity drops when seasonal background rates shift drastically; models rely on validation-selected operating thresholds ($p^* \approx 0.311268$ for Hyderabad, $p^* \approx 0.713448$ for India) rather than naive $0.50$.
  • Static Presentation Model: The website presentation layer visualizes precomputed R artifacts and does not support on-the-fly model re-training in JavaScript.

Data Sources

  • Air Quality: OpenAQ v3 REST API (Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations)
  • Meteorology: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (hourly temperature, humidity, wind speed, wind direction)
  • Regulatory Reference: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) National Air Quality Index standards
