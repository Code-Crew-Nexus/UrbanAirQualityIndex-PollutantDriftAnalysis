CPCB Methodology & Missingness Policy: AQI is calculated via the verified 3-pollutant subset (PM2.5, PM10, and daily maximum rolling 8-hour ozone `o3_8h_max`). Under project sufficiency rules, if any of these three pollutants lacks valid daily coverage, the composite AQI is recorded as missing (null) and omitted from descriptive averages. Unverified gases (CO, NO2, SO2) are strictly excluded from composite AQI due to upstream unit ambiguities. All metrics reflect frozen historical study data; no real-time or live monitoring is implied.
