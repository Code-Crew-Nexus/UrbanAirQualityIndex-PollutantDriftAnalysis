Bar chart comparing the rolling 30-day recent window mean pollutant concentration against the preceding 90-day baseline mean, filtered by station and pollutant variable. Used to assess magnitude of concentration shift for drift classification.

Drift Metric Interpretation Caution: The standardized mean shift Dz = (x̄recent − x̄baseline) / sbaseline divides the observed window difference by the baseline standard deviation (sbaseline), not pooled variance. Dz serves as a descriptive measure of magnitude and is not a hypothesis-test z-statistic. Magnitude thresholds strictly adhere to frozen empirical cutoffs: Minimal (|Dz| < 0.5), Mild (0.5 ≤ |Dz| < 1.0), Moderate (1.0 ≤ |Dz| < 2.0), and Strong (|Dz| ≥ 2.0).

Scientific Distinction Policy: Particulate variables (PM2.5, PM10), daily maximum rolling 8-hour ozone (`o3_8h_max`), and composite AQI represent verified physical units. Gaseous pollutants (CO, NO2, SO2) are evaluated strictly as within-series source-scale quantities to detect temporal shifts without converting to regulatory concentrations or applying CPCB AQI breakpoints. Findings indicate statistical associations within observed time-series and do not infer atmospheric causality.
